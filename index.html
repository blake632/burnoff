The 74-second tour

How a day goes

You live your life. The city does the busywork.

Here is what happens when a new customer emails you at 9 at night.

  1. The Mail Room reads it in minutes.It sorts every email: customers, questions, receipts, newsletters, junk.
  2. Leads writes the reply in your voice.It only uses facts you gave it. Missing one? It asks you instead of guessing.
  3. An audience tests it first.96 simulated customers react. A weak draft gets rewritten before you ever see it.
  4. You wake up and tap Send.Not quite right? Write what to change and it rewrites it. Once it has learned your way, turn on autopilot.
